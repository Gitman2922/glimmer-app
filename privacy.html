Glimmer privacy policy

Glimmer is a personal app used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it reads

Only with the owner's sign-in, Glimmer reads the owner's own Gmail (messages and their details), using Google's read-only Gmail permission. It never sends, changes, deletes or moves email.

How it is used

Glimmer's AI assistant reads the owner's email to tell the owner what needs a reply, what has a deadline and what can wait, and to prepare drafts the owner can copy. The AI services it uses are ones the owner has signed into and chosen (Claude or ChatGPT).

Where the data stays

What Glimmer saves stays on the owner's computer, encrypted. Nothing is sold, shared with other people, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Disconnecting an account in Glimmer deletes its saved sign-in and email details from the computer and tells Google to revoke the access. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data

Glimmer's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
